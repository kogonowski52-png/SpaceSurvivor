# Google Play listing · English

## App title

Galaxy Strike: Helion Gate

## Short description

A vertical space shooter with 10 missions, three bosses and four starships.

## Full description

Fly a starfighter through the rings and nebulae of Helion. In Galaxy Strike, steer with one finger while your ship fires automatically. Break enemy formations, dodge marked attack lanes and collect weapon modules as you fly.

CHART THE HELION ROUTE

Clear 10 missions across four regions. Start at Belt Echo, fight past Titan’s Graveyard and unlock the Heart of the Abyss. Replay completed missions to improve your score and earn up to three stars on each stage.

FACE THREE FLEET COMMANDERS

Dodge Asterion’s firing curtains, take down Vesperon’s batteries and fly through Nadir’s four attack phases. Watch the warning lanes and strike at vulnerable ship modules.

BUILD YOUR LOADOUT

Raise your firepower to rank seven. Pick up Laser Cannon, Plasma Spread, Homing Rockets and Energy Beam modules during missions. Unlock Peregrine, Aegis, Specter and Aurora. Fit equipment and upgrade hull, drive and weapons in the hangar.

Earn Credits, materials and Crystals through play. The main campaign and progress saves are available offline. Optional rewarded ads require a connection and an available ad.

The current in-game interface is in Polish.

## What’s new
