Galaxy Strike Privacy Policy

This policy applies from [[EFFECTIVE_DATE]]. The operator and data controller for Galaxy Strike, package com.galaxystrike.spacesurvivor, is [[OPERATOR_NAME_AND_ADDRESS]]. For privacy enquiries, contact [[PRIVACY_EMAIL]].

Gameplay and local data

Mission results, settings, unlocked ships and currency earned in the game are stored on your device. The campaign works offline without an account. Android lets you clear app data in Settings. Clearing app data deletes the local save and may make device-only progress unrecoverable.

Ads and privacy choices

Galaxy Strike offers optional rewarded ads through Google Mobile Ads. Google User Messaging Platform may contact Google at launch to determine which notices it must display and to check a saved choice. You choose whether to request a rewarded ad and the SDK checks whether your privacy status allows an ad request.

Google may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, ad and app interactions, diagnostics, and available device or service identifiers. The purposes include ad delivery, measurement, fraud prevention and consent management. Personalisation depends on your choice, region and SDK configuration. When the SDK requires a way to change your choice, use Settings → Audio → Ad privacy options in the Polish game interface. How Google processes data.

Optional test wallet and purchases

Real-money purchases are disabled in the basic test build. If the operator provides a licensed-test build and assigns you a code, you can connect a test account to the wallet. The wallet verifies the code over HTTPS. It stores a pseudonymous account identifier, installation identifier, product and purchase token, settlements, entitlements and refunds to verify, settle and restore purchases and prevent abuse. Google processes payment details in Google Play. The game and wallet backend do not receive your full card number.

This build can connect a test account issued by the operator. Follow [[DATA_REQUEST_URL]] to disconnect your account or request data deletion. The operator will verify your identity privately. Transaction records may be retained where needed for legal duties or to reconcile refunds.

Diagnostics and analytics

Gameplay and shop events stay in the current session’s memory. The game does not send them to an external analytics provider. The debug APK offers an optional export of FPS, memory and ad states without account, order or ad-session identifiers. You choose whether and where to share that report.
