## Progress and preferences

The game stores equipment, rewards, balances, mission progress, and sound preferences on the device. Missions and local progression can use offline storage. Clearing app data or uninstalling removes the local save. Google Play can restore supported permanent entitlements after you link your account. Currency balances remain local after delivery to the device.

## Advertising

The game offers optional rewarded ads. Depending on its configuration, Google Mobile Ads may use IP addresses to estimate approximate location, app and ad interactions, diagnostics, and device or advertising identifiers. These data are used for ad delivery, measurement, and fraud prevention. Google UMP manages consent forms for relevant regions. When UMP requires a privacy entry point, use **Settings → Ad privacy options** in the game. [How Google uses data](https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites)

## Purchases and purchase accounts

Google Play processes payments. The app and verification service process a pseudonymous account ID, a random installation ID, the purchase token and product ID, the purchase status, and delivery confirmation. These records support verification, entitlement recovery, and prevention of duplicate rewards. Google Play handles card information. Linking an account sends its individual activation code to the publisher's service over HTTPS. [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy)
