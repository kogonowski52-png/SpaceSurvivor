# Store listing · English

## App name

Galaxy Strike: Space Survivor

## Short description

Pilot a starfighter, upgrade your weapons, and survive enemy fleets.

## Full description

Take the controls of the Peregrine and fly into the dark side of the Helion system. Galaxy Strike: Space Survivor pairs one-finger flight controls with automatic weapons. Thread your way through enemy fire, collect experience, and choose upgrades in the middle of each battle.

- Survive in three sectors. Sorties last up to ten minutes and lead to battles against command ships.
- Combine six weapon systems. Missiles, drones, plasma, beams, and lightning let you build different loadouts.
- Develop your hangar. Unlock four ships, equip modules, and upgrade your hull, drive, and weapons.
- Open salvage crates, complete objectives, and collect rewards from your sorties.

Missions and local progression are playable offline. Rewarded ads and store offers depend on connectivity and Google Play configuration. The current in-game interface is in Polish.
